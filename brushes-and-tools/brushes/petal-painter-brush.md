# Petal Painter Brush

### `//ezbr` <mark style="color:orange;">`petalpainter`</mark>

<details>

<summary><mark style="color:blue;">Petal Painter Brush</mark></summary>

**`//ezbr petalpainter`** <mark style="color:orange;">**`<type>`**</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">**`[radius]`**</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">**`[density]`**</mark> <mark style="color:orange;">**`[-n <noise>] [-z <scale>] [-m <mask>]`**</mark>

Places foliage (Leaves, Wildflowers, Pink Petals) above desired blocks.

* <mark style="color:orange;">**Type**</mark>: Sets the foliage type.
* <mark style="color:orange;">**Radius**</mark> (Default: 8): Sets the radius of the brush.
* <mark style="color:orange;">**Density**</mark> (Default: 0.3): Sets how abundant the foliage is.
* <mark style="color:orange;">**`-n <noise>`**</mark> (Default: `White()`): Adds an underlying noise field.
* <mark style="color:orange;">**`-z <scale>`**</mark> (Default: 0.03): Modifies the scale of the noise.
* <mark style="color:orange;">**`-m <mask>`**</mark> (Default: `#fullblock`): Sets the blocks to place on top of

</details>
